# Public demo capture sources

- `site1`: **Accessible University — Inaccessible Version**, University of
  Washington AccessComputing, https://projects.accesscomputing.uw.edu/au/before.html.
  The source identifies its license as [Creative Commons Attribution-
  NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
  The cached screenshot-based video and extracted content retain that attribution
  and license. The page is a deliberately inaccessible fictional training site,
  not the university's actual home page. Added material consists of accessibility
  annotations and Romanian narration generated by ClearPath.
- `site2`: **Drudge Report**, https://www.drudgereport.com/, captured on 2026-11-06
  for this accessibility demonstration. Third-party page content and branding
  belong to their respective owners. The clip adds accessibility annotations and
  generated narration; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the source.
- `bad-checkout`: the repository's Romanian test fixture.

These are point-in-time captures. The reports describe the captured pages and
the configured automated audit, not an enduring rating of their publishers.
